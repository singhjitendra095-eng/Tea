# User Personas
## The Tea Story

Version: 1.0

Status: Approved

Owner: Product Team

---

# Purpose

This document defines the primary customer personas for The Tea Story.

These personas should guide every product decision, including:

- User Experience (UX)
- User Interface (UI)
- Product Features
- Content Strategy
- Marketing Messaging
- Personalization
- AI Recommendations
- Customer Support
- Future Product Roadmap

Every new feature should identify which persona(s) it primarily serves.

## Persona 1 – The Wellness Professional

### Profile

**Name:** Priya Sharma

**Age:** 32

**Occupation:** IT Consultant

**Location:** Bengaluru

**Income:** ₹18–30 LPA

**Lifestyle**

- Busy corporate life
- Works long hours
- Exercises regularly
- Practices yoga
- Values mental wellness
- Shops online frequently

---

### Goals

- Replace sugary beverages with healthier alternatives
- Improve daily wellness
- Discover premium products
- Save time while shopping

---

### Pain Points

- Too many low-quality tea brands
- Difficult to trust ingredient quality
- Generic product descriptions
- Lack of personalization

---

### Buying Behaviour

- Purchases every month
- Reads ingredients carefully
- Values product reviews
- Comfortable paying premium prices
- Mobile-first shopper

---

### Success Criteria

The customer quickly finds a tea that aligns with her wellness goals, understands its benefits, customizes it to her preference, and completes checkout with confidence.

# Design Implications

The product experience should emphasize:

- Large product imagery
- Elegant typography
- Calm colour palette
- Minimal distractions
- Premium storytelling
- Rich product details
- Clear customization flow
- Smooth transitions
- Fast page loading
- Trust-building throughout the journey
